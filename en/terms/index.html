The service

Raceport is an app that brings together race calendars and results in sports such as road running, trail, triathlon, HYROX, cycling and open water, lets you follow your race career, and connects you with other athletes and communities.

Raceport is not a race organiser or a timing company. Plans in Raceport only show that you intend to take part in a race; they are not an official race registration.

We may change Raceport's features over time, add new ones or remove some.

Some features require membership; without signing in you can use only the features offered to guests.

You can delete your account at any time. See the Account Deletion page for details.

Community rules

To keep Raceport safe and respectful for everyone, you must follow these rules. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.

  • Do not post content that harasses, threatens or bullies others, or that contains hate speech or discrimination.
  • Do not post obscene, violent or illegal content.
  • Do not impersonate another person or organisation; do not claim a race result that is not yours.
  • Do not post spam, advertising or misleading content.
  • Do not share other people's personal information without their permission.
  • Do not try to disrupt Raceport, get around its security measures or collect its data by automated means without permission.

As a club admin, you are responsible for the announcements you write on behalf of the community.

Reporting, blocking and moderation

You can report a profile or an announcement that you find breaks the rules from within the app. Reports are reviewed by our editors.

When you block a user, you no longer see each other's profiles, plans or presence in lists, and any follows between you are removed.

We may remove content that breaks the rules without prior notice, delete profile information, and close the accounts of users who break the rules.

Race data

Race calendars and results are imported from third-party sources such as the public pages of organisers and timing companies. This data may contain errors, gaps or delays; we do not guarantee that it is accurate or up to date. The official result is the one published by the organiser.

You can claim that a result is yours. A similar name alone is not proof; claims are reviewed by our editors, who may ask for more information. False claims are rejected and may lead to the account being closed.

If you see an error in a result, you can tell us at yasir.kavras@albertsoftware.com.
