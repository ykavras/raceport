Find your race, build your career.

Discover running, trail, HYROX, triathlon and open water races and club meetups by city and sport. See who is going, and let your official results go on your career.

01Discover

Your next race is here.

Browse races and club meetups by city and sport, and see them all at a glance on the event map.

  • By city and sport
  • Event map
  • Meetups near you

02My plan

See who is going.

Say “I’m going” and the event lands in your plan. See where the people you follow are going, and choose who sees your plan.

Who sees your plan?

  • Participants
  • My followers
  • Only me

03Career

Your official results, on your career.

Results come from the organizer’s official list. Pick your race, find your result and say “This is me”; once approved, it’s added to your career and personal bests.

  • Official result lists
  • Personal bests
  • Career achievements

04Stats

Find your place in the tables.

Tables fill with official results from timing companies that allow rankings. See the season leaders in your sport and format, and where you stand.

  • Within sport and format
  • Season leaders
  • Official times only

05Communities

Running together is easier.

Find your club and join; see its meetups in My plan and never miss an announcement. You can join open meetups without being a member.

  • Club meetups
  • Announcements
  • Join without being a member

06Calendar

In your calendar, always up to date.

Add an event to your calendar in one tap, or link your plan to your phone’s calendar as a live subscription; when a date changes, your calendar follows.

Add to calendar

07Follows and notifications

Never miss a change.

Follow runners and clubs. Hear about it when a race in your plan changes, your result is approved or your club posts an announcement.

  • A race in your plan changed date
  • Your result was approved
  • New announcement from your club
  • New follower
